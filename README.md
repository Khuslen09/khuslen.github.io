# https://lenlab.pages.dev

> LENLAB — Knowledge. Curated.

 LENLAB is a personal knowledge lab and content platform founded in 2026 by [Khuslen Chuluunbat](https://lenlab.pages.dev/). It started as a space to share insights on computer science, business, and life, and has since evolved into a multilingual hub connecting readers across Korea, Mongolia, and the world.

### 🌐 Multilingual Platform

LENLAB bridges three languages to make knowledge accessible to everyone:

* **Korean (한국어):** Main hub for detailed articles and community discussions.
* **Mongolian (Монгол):** Insights and perspectives tailored for the Mongolian audience.
* **English:** Global insights and technical deep dives for international readers.

### 🚀 What You’ll Find

* **Computer Science:** Deep dives into algorithms, system design, and modern development practices.
* **Business & Strategy:** Frameworks for growth, product thinking, and startup insights.
* **Life & Philosophy:** Personal reflections on learning, habits, and meaningful living.
* **Reading Notes:** Curated summaries and key takeaways from books and research.

### 🌟 The Vision

LENLAB is built on three core principles:

1. **Accessibility:** Breaking down complex topics into easy-to-understand content.
2. **Connection:** Building a global community of learners and thinkers.
3. **Impact:** Empowering individuals with the knowledge to build better products and lives.

### ✨ Features

* **Dual-Mode Design:** Toggle between dark and light modes for comfortable reading.
* **Interactive Elements:** Custom cursor effects, hover animations, and smooth scrolling.
* **Responsive Layout:** Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* **Search & Navigation:** Easy access to content across all languages.

### 🤝 Get Connected

Whether you’re a student, professional, or lifelong learner, LENLAB is a space for you. Explore the latest posts, join the conversation, and let’s build the future of knowledge together.
